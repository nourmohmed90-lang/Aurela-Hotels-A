# Aurelia Hotels Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time is check-in?
Standard check-in starts at 3:00 PM.

### What time is check-out?
Standard check-out is at 12:00 PM.

### Is free Wi-Fi available?
Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and public areas.

### Are pets allowed?
Pets are allowed only in designated pet-friendly rooms.

### Is breakfast included?
Breakfast depends on the selected booking package.

### Can I request a room upgrade?
Yes, upgrades are subject to availability.

### Does the hotel offer airport transportation?
Airport shuttle service is available at selected branches.

### How can I contact customer support?
Guests can contact the Front Desk or the Customer Service department 24/7.
