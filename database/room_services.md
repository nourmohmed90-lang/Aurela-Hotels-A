# Aurelia Hotels Room Services

## Purpose
This document lists the room services available to hotel guests.

## Available Services
- Daily housekeeping
- Room service dining
- Laundry service
- Wake-up calls
- Airport transportation
- Extra towels and pillows
- Baby crib upon request
- Luggage assistance

## Premium Services
- Executive lounge access
- Private airport transfer
- Express laundry
- Concierge assistance

## Service Rules
- Some services may require additional charges.
- Premium services are available only for eligible room categories.
- Service availability may vary by hotel branch.

## Defensive Design Rules
- Verify guest identity before fulfilling requests.
- Record all chargeable services.
- Reject unavailable service requests.
