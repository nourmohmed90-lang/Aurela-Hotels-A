# Aurelia Hotels VIP Guest Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the benefits and service priorities for VIP guests.

## VIP Benefits
- Priority check-in and check-out.
- Complimentary room upgrades when available.
- Dedicated customer support.
- Welcome amenities upon arrival.
- Flexible compensation decisions when service recovery is required.

## Service Rules
- VIP requests should receive higher processing priority.
- Upgrade availability depends on occupancy.
- Staff should minimize waiting time for VIP guests.

## Defensive Design Rules
- Verify VIP status before applying benefits.
- Do not assign unavailable premium rooms.
- Record every complimentary upgrade.
