# Aurelia Hotels Employee Guidelines

## Purpose
This document outlines the responsibilities and expected conduct for hotel employees.

## Employee Responsibilities
- Treat every guest with professionalism and respect.
- Follow hotel policies during every interaction.
- Protect guest privacy and confidential information.
- Report operational issues immediately.
- Escalate unresolved guest complaints to supervisors.

## Service Standards
- Respond politely to guest inquiries.
- Resolve issues whenever possible during the first interaction.
- Keep accurate records of guest requests.

## Defensive Design Rules
- Employees may only perform actions within their assigned role.
- Managers are required for high-value approvals.
- All policy exceptions must be documented.
